What is OC? Original Campaign (or OC for short) is a Dungeons and Dragons campaign my friend group is doing. However, it isn't your typical "Slay the dragon" or "Save the princess" plotline. The plot came about due to restrictions, at the time some people in my friend group couldn't play D&D due to restrictive parents. So, we came up with a workaround. We made our own engine and plot. The engine is discussed in the engine page, so what is the plot and how did it get to where it is? Well, we ultimately decided to use what he had in front of us instead of starting fresh. Many people in my friend group have their own original characters, these could be from their own world, characters in a world from a franchise they like or even a mixture of the two. We decided to use these characters as the players characters. With this in mind, we needed to come up with a plot surrounding these characters, and we came up with this:
Plot The Evil Queen Mary Sue has destroyed the players' character's worlds and combined them into one amalgamation-reality called "Headcannon", with her own castle in the center of it all. The starting players: Jestr, Socks, Damian and Athena wake up in something called "PI containment center" after causing mayhem, (as a typical D&D party does) they escape and find out that PI stands for Plot Immunity. They soon learn that they need to collect the McGuffins scattered across the warped remains of their own worlds to break Mary Sue's Plot Armor and return to the status quo. Along the way they meet more characters like Buddy, Glen-4, Tori, Alex, Claypi and more.
So, What is AU? This project is very ambitious, so ambitious in fact that we needed to divide it into 2 separate campaigns. The second campaign goes by "Original Campaign: Alternate Universe". (Or AU for short) The plot is generally the same with some differences, mainly Main cast. AU's players include Blitz, Faelyn, Roma, Ghost, Shibe, and Shadow-1. Just having different people can lead to completely new situations.
