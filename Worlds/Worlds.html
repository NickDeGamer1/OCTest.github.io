Worlds Each character's world has been taken over by one of Mary Sue's generals. They have reformed reality into their own image. Now the characters must explore the remains in the hopes of finding the McGuffin. As revealed after chapter 2, the character's world returns to what it was before the takeover once they acquire that worlds McGuffin. There haven't been too many chapters of OC/AU so we will only describe the ones that have happened, any characters that are not mentioned simply haven't appeared but are confirmed. (There are also even more than listed here, believe it or not)
Jestr's Chapter: CWC trip to Hell The players end up in Jestr's hometown, The Pride circle of Hell. It has been taken over by Soncihu. (so not much changed) Hell went from a run-down city filled with crime and hate to a colorful, whimsical, and poorly drawn suburb (what a nightmare). The players explore the city and put a stop to Sonichu's shenanigans.
Alex's Chapter: When the Starrs align The players are sent to the Hoenn region to find another PI that wasn't captured By Mary Sue's legion. Soon after arriving, they meet Alex. They come to find hes a tad strange, He often fiddles with his bandana and is very protective of his pet, Aurora. Despite this, they follow him in his search for his missing sister. Eventually they meet Starr, alpha of the werewolf pack. She and the rest of the pack labels Alex as a "White Wolf", whatever that means...
