Aurora

Aurora is an Alolan Vulpix Alex rescued from Team Aqua. She is very short tempered and will accept any challenge to battle. Her dream is to be a champion; however, she has a disability where her body is ultra-sensitive to heat. Because of this she wears the cooling vest.
