Tori

Victoria is based on the "sold to a celebrity" self-insert fanfiction trope. She is fairly shallow and kind of vain, but she tries to be pleasant because others are useful to her. Victoria fools and manipulates people with charm and looks. She does not lack intelligence, but her brains are not her greatest asset. Victoria is not physically strong by any means; she requires the support of others to get by and stay safe. She makes herself useful by healing and providing other aid to her team. Victoria was married off to an important government official as a teenager. Her family received a large sum of money for this. Her late husband was elderly and recently died. She resents him and her family for this situation. She learns herbalism and becomes a rogue healer.
