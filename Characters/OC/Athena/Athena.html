Athena

Physically strong; Personality similar to a combo of Undyne's and Urbosa's; Girlfriend is Violet; Additional party members in original universe are Garlic and Dash; Overthinks her actions; Feels shameful for being emotional; Essentially a cyberpunk/fantasy Ranger; She's essentially a Dragon/Psychic type, in terms of element-based aspects. Controls weapons via computer implants, which are her universe's version of smartphones, as technology is greatly advanced ahead of ours in most cases. In addition, these implants handle monetary transactions, personal info, communication, personal devices, location related processes, etc. In addition, magic and technology are one in Athena's universe, hence why spells, ESP, monsters, etc. are commonplace.
