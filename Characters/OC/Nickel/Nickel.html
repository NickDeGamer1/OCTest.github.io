Nickel

Nickel is an Umbreon from "The Land Across the Sky". He generally tends to himself, not intervening on what anyone else is doing. In his homeplace, he owns and lives in a library, despite not knowing how to read (he will deny this of course). The party found him in Alex's basement during the events of OC. He joined Alex's team because they have a history.
