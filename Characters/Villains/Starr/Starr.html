Starr
Alignment
Caotic Nutral

Starr is a sassy werewolf general in Mary Sue's army. She thinks she is the best person in the room and will make sure you know that. She frequently attempts to degrade the players' characters with sarcasm and backhanded comments. Just don't call her a furry, or else.
