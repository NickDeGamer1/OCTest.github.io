Penta

As a young pentagon in the 2nd dimension, Penta was always a little different. Instead of going off to war against the circles like all pentagons were at her age, she instead wanted to do some exploring in the mines. As she went exploring, she quickly found a rouge band of circles upon her! She ran as fast as she could, straight into a triangle. She was imbued with the power of the triangles and henceforth given a more humanoid form. Penta is still finding herself and her meaning in the world, and because most fandoms' dimensions are very separated from hers, she only recently discovered that AUs existed. She has a slightly withheld personality. Penta enjoys a good meal and strives to achieve culinary success. Penta's combat style relies on her extensive arms, as well as backup from her friends to land hits, as her attacks travel fairly slowly
