Jimothy

Jimothy is a failed laboratory experiment, however not in a traditional sense. He is a two-foot-long axolotl with a human lifespan and less than mediocre human intelligence, who was abandoned by his creators and shot into orbit. After twenty years, he had returned to a futuristic, Utopian Earth. With nothing particularly special besides his complicated past and trauma from scientists, he tries. He can't hit hard, he can't defend himself, and he's fairly weak, but he's very cute! :D
