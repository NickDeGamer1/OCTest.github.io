Blitz

Blitz wanted to make a lot of money so that she and her family (but mainly herself) could live comfortably and healthily later on. The fastest way to do that was to become a streamer. A streamer in this sense is a person who fights the many monsters that are in the world and livestreams it to nobles and middle-class businesses that could afford to do a sponsorship. Sponsorship is required to become a streamer. Blitz got her sponsorship from her childhood friend's parent's restaurant/sort of pub (runs 24 hours) that just barely meets the business requirements to sponsor. There are two other streamers sponsored by them: a quirky, annoying, bouncy wind magic half elf girl who is the adult men�s favorite to ogle at, and a stoic knight type guy who tends to get in way over his head. Blitz, being the newest streamer of the three tends to get the late nighttime block. She is the least popular of the three and has some self-confidence issues but will gladly point out someone's bs. She is known for her hit and run tactics that look like a laser light show. She developed this technique in hopes that she could still make an interesting stream even in the least busy time slot.
