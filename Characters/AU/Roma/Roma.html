Roma

Hailing from the story, "Second Soul", Alexander "Roma" Romana had grown up a religious Christian and passionate boxer. His Awakening, King of Mercy, had arisen from the gem on his pendant during his debut professional boxing match from his will to win. Since that fight, Roma learned his Awakening's ability to block any attack he sees coming. He has above average strength, speed, agility, and stamina, as well as an Awakening with an indestructible tail; however, he falls short when his anger gets the best of him. He is very well respected by the members of his gym- not by his words, but through the resolve he shows.
