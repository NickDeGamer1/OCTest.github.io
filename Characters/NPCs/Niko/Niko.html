Niko
Alignment
Lawful Good

Niko is an Ex-coworker of Buddy and Misty. He is the combat medic, being able to revive anybody using his magnetic collar. Unfortunately, he might not know when someone is knocked out or just lying down. he also has no combat experience, so bringing him along is a huge risk.
