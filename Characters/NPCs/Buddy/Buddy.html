Buddy
Alignment
Lawful Neutral

Buddy is a Robotic mutt that leads the OC crew. He is their "Dog in the chair" that provides all the mission details needed for the team. He himself is rather old, replacing his failing limbs with robotic counterparts. No one knows why he is helping the crew, but it seems... personal.
