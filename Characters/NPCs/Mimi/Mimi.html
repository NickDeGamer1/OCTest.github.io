Mimi
Alignment
True Nutral

Dawning his rainbow afro he got from a children’s MMO, Mimi is the meme dealer that follows the OC and AU crews. He will constantly blabber about random topics to the point of insanity (at least in Buddy’s case). He will seemingly appear at random and offer to sell the players items. These items range from “The Real Zenith from Terraria!!!1!” to “The Box” (don’t open the box).
